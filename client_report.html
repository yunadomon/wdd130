Client & Developer Team Project - Report

Client

I decided on the content and images because I love bread and wanted a site that is visually clean and makes visitors feel hungry. I was able to communicate my vision for the images clearly to my developer. However, achieving the perfect look was challenging at times. For instance, some colors didn't quite match the site's atmosphere. Through this process, I learned the importance of constant communication to find the best solutions. In the end, my developer met my expectations, and I am satisfied with the result. For future projects, I will specify exactly which colors and fonts should be used for each specific part of the site. This will help both the developer and me achieve the perfect design.

Lead Developer

I maintained excellent communication with my client throughout the project. First, I built the site based on the wireframe created by the client. After showing her the initial version, she provided constructive feedback and shared her overall vision. Her advice was very clear, which made it easy for me to implement the necessary improvements. For the launch, I provided the published site to the client. This allows the public to access it while ensuring the client cannot accidentally modify the code themselves. Managing the project with GitHub was highly efficient. I found it very convenient because any changes I made were reflected quickly, allowing us to see the updated version immediately.

View

General Experience and Reflections

One of the biggest challenges I faced was effectively communicating my vision; even with a clear idea in mind, it was often difficult to translate it into words without slight misunderstandings. I overcame this by consistently confirming progress during every class to stay aligned. The most rewarding part of the project was acting as the developer and using CSS to bring the client’s wireframes to life; it was incredibly satisfying to see a cluttered layout gradually transform into a clean, polished website. If I were to do this again, I would ensure my wireframes as a client were much more specific regarding fonts and colors, while as a developer, I would prepare proactive color suggestions to help bridge the gap between initial concepts and the final look. This experience has significantly boosted my confidence for future real-world projects. Having never built a website before, successfully navigating the complexities of incorporating a client's specific requests while completing a functional site was an invaluable milestone for me.

Yuna Domon
